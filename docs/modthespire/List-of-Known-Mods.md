# List of Known Mods

> **⚠️ THIS LIST IS NOW OUT OF DATE** (as of January 3rd, 2019)
>
> Official mods are now found on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/app/646570/workshop/), though older mods from this list can still be downloaded and used manually.

## Major Categories

### API Mods
Foundation tools including BaseMod, Friendly Minions, and StSLib that enable modding functionality.

### New Characters
Approximately 25 custom playable characters including:
- The Seeker
- The Valiant
- The Witch
- The Construct
- The Glutton (emphasizes self-inflicted damage and gold scaling)
- And many others, each with unique mechanics and card pools

### Cards/Relics
Content expansion mods like:
- Replay The Spire
- Hubris
- Conspire

### Other
Utility and cosmetic modifications including:
- SuperFastMode
- Relic Sorter
- AscensionPlus

### Outdated Mods
No longer maintained entries like:
- Lotus Cards
- Black Mage

## External Tools

Non-mod utilities:
- STS deck builders
- Card image creation tool for modders

*For the most current list of mods, please visit the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/app/646570/workshop/).*
